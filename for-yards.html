How listings are built

Base offering, included services, optional extras.

Pick your accommodation type, then the services that come with it as standard, then anything owners can add for an extra fee. Equivoa turns that into a clear package on your listing. The complexity lives in your setup, not in what the owner has to read.

A
Base offering
e.g. "Stable + turnout" or "Grass grazing": the foundation of what you're renting out.
B
Included services
What comes as standard: 2x turnout, hay, mucking out, daily checks, arena access, whatever's actually true for your yard.
C
Optional extras
Anything owners can add on top, each with its own price: extra turnout, a weekly lesson, clipping.
