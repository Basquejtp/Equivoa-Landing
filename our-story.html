Why Equivoa exists.

Every good idea starts with a problem someone got tired of living with. Here's ours.

Jack Costello, founder

I started Equivoa after seeing how difficult it can still be to find the right place for a horse. Too often, the search means scrolling through Facebook groups, asking around, sending messages and hoping someone knows of a space that might work.

Coming from a farming background and with a genuine interest in the equestrian world, I wanted to explore whether there was a better way to connect the people who have space with the people looking for it.

That is where Equivoa began. The idea is simple: make it easier for horse owners to discover paddocks, yards and equestrian spaces that actually suit their needs, while giving landowners and equestrian businesses a better way to make their spaces visible.

Equivoa is still early. I am building it alongside the people who will actually use it, listening to horse owners, landowners and equestrian businesses to understand what works, what does not, and what is genuinely worth building.

If you have a question, an idea, or simply want to tell me about your experience finding equestrian space, I would genuinely like to hear from you.

Jack Costello
Founder, Equivoa
