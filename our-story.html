Our story

Why Equivoa exists.

Every good idea starts with a problem someone got tired of living with. Here's ours.

[Your name], founder

I started Equivoa because finding the right yard for a horse should not mean weeks of unanswered messages, half-updated Facebook groups and phone calls that all start the same way. Anyone who has kept a horse in the UK knows this problem. It is not a small one, and nobody had really fixed it.

I wanted to build something that treats horse owners like people with real priorities, not a list of boxes to tick, and that treats yards and coaches like the small businesses they are, not line items in someone else's marketplace.

Equivoa is still early. What you see here is a working first version, built in public, with the first Founding Farms shaping how it grows. If that sounds like you, I would love to hear from you.
