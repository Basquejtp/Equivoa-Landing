How it works

No endless filters.
No scrolling through irrelevant listings.

Most search tools ask you to build a query. Equivoa asks what matters to you, then does the searching, and stays honest about places that almost fit.

1
Tell us what matters
Turnout, care, facilities, training, the feel of the yard: a short set of honest questions, grouped the way you'd actually think about them, not a database form.
2
Build your Equivoa profile
Mark what's a genuine must-have versus what's just nice to have. Your profile is an understanding of your priorities, not a rigid checklist, and you control what you share.
3
Discover your best matches
Every listing gets scored against your priorities and explained in plain English, like "2x turnout ✓, indoor arena ✓, weekly instruction not offered", not a mysterious percentage.
4
Compare what each place actually offers
Near-matches stay visible. If a yard is missing one thing you asked for, we tell you, and let you decide if it still matters once you've seen the rest.
5
Enquire or book
Straightforward listings book directly. Complex or bespoke arrangements go to enquiry or a quote, because not every yard can be booked like a hotel room, and we don't pretend otherwise.
The philosophy

Your Equivoa profile makes your search better. You control what you're comfortable sharing.
