About Equivoa

Equestrian accommodation is more complicated than a checkbox.

Every horse owner who's tried to find a yard knows the drill: a Facebook group, a dozen unanswered messages, a phone call that starts with "so what do you actually offer?" We think there's a better way to connect people who need the right setup with the people providing it.

"Equivoa doesn't make horse owners search for a yard. It learns what they're looking for, and surfaces the closest setups — even when nothing matches perfectly."
01
Why we started with paddocks, stables & riding schools
Three sides of the same problem — owners need somewhere to keep a horse, and somewhere to learn to ride it better — and no single place to look for either.
02
Why we don't force yards into three tiers
"DIY / Part / Full" means something different at every yard in the country. We'd rather ask what's actually included and let owners judge for themselves.
03
Where we're going
Connecting demand with supply, more usefully over time — understanding what owners actually want and where the market isn't yet meeting it, while never losing sight of the individual match in front of you.
