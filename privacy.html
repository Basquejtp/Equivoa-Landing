Privacy

Your profile makes your search better.
You control what you share.

This page describes the principles Equivoa is being built around. It isn't a substitute for a full legal privacy policy — that gets written and reviewed properly before real accounts and real data collection go live.

01
We don't sell identifiable profiles
Your preferences exist to get you better matches. They are not a product we sell to third parties.
02
You choose what you share
Every preference on your profile is optional. Fewer answers means broader, less tailored matches — not a locked door.
03
Aggregated insight, not individual tracking
Where we look at patterns across many owners — say, where demand for indoor arenas outstrips supply in a region — that analysis is designed to work on aggregated, anonymised data, not on tracking you individually.
04
Anything beyond this needs your consent
Any future use of your data beyond making your own search better — for example, a relevant equestrian business reaching people who've expressed interest in that category — would be explicit and permission-based, not automatic or hidden in fine print.
