Why Equivoa

The right setup isn't always the one with the most boxes ticked.

Equestrian accommodation is complicated — a yard might mix stabling, grazing, care levels and training in dozens of combinations. So instead of forcing every yard into "DIY / Part / Full", Equivoa lets providers describe what they actually offer, and matches you on what matters to you specifically.

01
Tell us what matters
A few honest questions about turnout, care, facilities and training — not a 40-field form. Mark what's a must-have and what's just nice to have.
02
See real matches, not a wall of listings
Every place is scored against what you asked for, with a plain-English reason why — including close matches that are missing one thing, because you might not mind.
03
Enquire, book, or ask for a quote
Simple listings book instantly. Complex or bespoke ones go to enquiry or a quote. We don't pretend every yard can be booked like a hotel room.
