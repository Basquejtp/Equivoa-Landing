How it works

From empty stable to booked stable.

i.

List what you've got

Yards and coaches add paddocks, boxes or lesson slots in minutes — photos, pricing, and real-time availability.

ii.

Riders find and book

Search by location, discipline and budget. No group chats, no waiting on a callback — just a booking, confirmed.

Equivoa charges a small transaction fee only when money changes hands. You set your own rates and rules, always.
